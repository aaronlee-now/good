📖 How to Play

🎯 Goal

Grow your economy, raise an army, and march on the enemy outpost to capture territory — all while keeping your base alive.

🕹️ Move

  • WASD / ↑↓←→ walk your commander · 📱 drag on the board for a joystick
  • ⚔️ Stand at the enemy outpost (top) to lead the assault
  • 🛠️ Stand at your base (bottom) to repair it
  • 🪙 Walk over dropped coins to grab bonus cash

⌨️ Build hotkeys

  • 12345 units
  • QERF economy buildings
  • T tower · G sell goods
  • Space start / resume
  • 🖱️ Or just click / 📱 tap any shop button.

💡 Tips

  • Build your economy first, then your army.
  • Sell goods when the market button glows green.
  • Capture outposts to grow — each one is tougher.

Mini War ⚔️

Build your war economy, then raise an army! Spend cash on Farms / Workshops / Banks to grow your income, then buy Soldiers, Tanks & Helis that march on the enemy outpost. Smash it to capture territory — each new region is tougher. Research deep tech chains to unlock the elite Commando, and Sell Goods when the market spikes. Walk your commander (WASD / arrows or drag to use the joystick) up to the outpost to lead the assault, back to base to repair, and over fallen coins to grab them. Don't let your base fall!

Economy first, then crush the outpost!
